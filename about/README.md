# About Creative Engines

**Creative Engines is a content intelligence and production platform, founded by Igor Levi and Ilya Molochnikov.** It exists to give a brand a reason its buyers choose it, and to keep that reason governed across everything the brand ships. This page is about the people who built it and why.

## Why Creative Engines exists

Creative Engines was built by a founder who was the bottleneck. As a marketer, Igor Levi could not delegate content without losing the brand's voice and quality, so every piece routed back through him – and he became the ceiling on his own company's output.

That personal problem turned out to be the market's problem. AI has made two things cheap: building products, and producing content. What stays scarce is direction – which audience, which message, and which content actually works, repeatably, as a system rather than a one-off deck on a shelf. The bottleneck moved from "can we make content?" to "can we make the *right* content without a senior person gating every piece?"

The founders' answer draws on a career-long conviction of Igor's: replace the founder's instinct with a repeatable, evidence-based system. Durable results come from systems, not from one operator working longer hours. Creative Engines is that system – brand truth, buyer understanding, and governed execution – supplying the judgment AI does not.

## Who founded Creative Engines

The pairing is the story, and it maps to the product. A governance layer for serious marketers has to be both strategically deep and technically real, so it was built by one of each – two operators who have spent decades building and running complex systems inside global organizations.

**Igor Levi – CEO and co-founder.** The marketing, product, and go-to-market half. Igor has spent more than 20 years shipping products to global enterprises across SaaS, AdTech, and FinTech, and has been certified in project management by the Project Management Institute (PMI) since 2004, in business analysis (BABOK), and in AI for Product Marketing (Product Marketing Alliance, 2025). He also holds a Johns Hopkins data-science specialization – nine courses including machine learning – so the audience-science behind Creative Engines is not something he outsourced. Two builds define the track record: he scaled **TUNE Pay** from zero to 200+ enterprise clients worldwide in a single year, and it is still in production six years later; and after a startup he co-founded was acquired by **Billups**, a global media holding, he built its entire Product Department and product strategy, and the product organization he stood up still runs there. Outside Creative Engines, he advises and shapes go-to-market strategy for startups. Based in Israel. [Connect on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/igor8levi/).

**Ilya Molochnikov – CTO and co-founder.** The technical half, and a builder of complex enterprise systems at scale. A former Executive Director at a major bank's R&D division, Ilya led machine-learning and AI programs and a large engineering group that grew into the thousands. His fields are AI and machine learning, big data, computer vision, large language models, and the mathematics and computer science beneath them, and he holds a master's degree from the Yandex School of Data Analysis. A founder since the early 2000s, he built two game studios – one to a successful exit – and today owns the platform-engineering layer of Creative Engines: its core engine, including the ability to run securely on a brand's own infrastructure, an enterprise-grade capability that matters to data-sensitive teams. Based in Israel. [Connect on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ilya-molochnikov/).

Not engineers guessing at marketing, and not marketers hand-waving at AI: a CEO who knows what "right" looks like at enterprise scale, paired with an R&D leader who knows how to make it fast and keep it governed.

## What Creative Engines believes

Creative Engines runs its own marketing on its own product – a go-to-market engine that has to go to market, on itself. That shapes what the company holds to:

- **Strategic partnership.** The product is a force multiplier for human judgment, not a replacement for it. It makes the people who use it sharper; it does not sideline them.
- **Human-in-the-loop control.** A person approves what represents the brand. Governance is a feature, not a friction.
- **Quality over price.** The company competes on strategic value and end-to-end integration, not on being the cheapest tool.
- **Intellectual-property security.** Each brand's knowledge stays isolated and owned by the brand – a competitive advantage kept, not leaked.

The mission that ties them together: help teams create strategy and content based on how their audience actually thinks and buys, so strategy stops sitting in a forgotten deck and starts driving every piece of content that ships.
