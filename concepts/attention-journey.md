# Attention Journey

The Attention Journey is the Creative Engines concept for mapping where a buyer's attention actually travels before they act, and what they are thinking and feeling at each point along the way.

Most teams describe the buyer path as a generic funnel: awareness, consideration, decision. That model is easy to draw but hard to use, because it does not say where a specific buyer is paying attention, in what order, or in what state of mind. Creative Engines treats the customer journey as a map of attention, not a row of funnel stages.

## From Mindset to journey

A [Mindset](./mindset-intelligence.md) explains who a buyer is and why they would care, resist, trust, or ignore a message. The Attention Journey takes that buyer state and follows it across the real surfaces where attention is won or lost.

For a given Mindset, the journey describes:

- Which touchpoints the buyer encounters, and in what sequence.
- Which media channels carry attention at each step, across dozens of possible surfaces such as search, social feeds, peer communities, video, email, review sites, events, and word of mouth.
- What the buyer is paying attention to at that moment, and what they are filtering out.
- What they currently believe when they arrive at that touchpoint.
- What emotion is in play, and what tension needs to resolve before they move forward.
- What evidence or framing would earn the next step of attention.

The result is a map. It shows attention as a path with branches and drop-off points, not a single straight line.

## Methods behind the map

The Attention Journey draws on established ways of thinking about buyer behavior rather than inventing a private vocabulary. Publicly describable influences include Jobs To Be Done, which frames the progress a buyer is trying to make, and Google's "Moments That Matter" research and the later micro-moments and messy-middle thinking, which study the specific points where attention and intent actually form.

Creative Engines treats these as inputs to a structured map, not as reading material. The buyer's jobs, moments, beliefs, and emotions become nodes and edges in a graph of attention, so a team can reason about the whole route instead of guessing channel by channel.

## Routing attention like a navigation system

A useful way to picture the Attention Journey is a navigation system planning a drive. A navigator does not just mark the start and the destination. It weighs the whole route: time, distance, effort, cost, and even toll roads, and then proposes the path most likely to arrive well.

Creative Engines plans attention the same way. Across the graph of touchpoints and channels, it can reason about the route a buyer's attention is most likely to take and where the brand should invest:

- **Time** maps to how long attention takes to move toward a decision.
- **Effort** maps to how much work the buyer must do at each step.
- **Cost** maps to the budget and creative effort a channel demands.
- **Toll roads** map to paid placements that can shortcut part of the route when they are worth it.

The output is a planned route through attention, not a static funnel diagram. As real behavior is observed, the route can be re-planned, the way a navigation system reroutes around traffic.

## Why a map beats a funnel

A funnel assumes every buyer moves through the same abstract stages at the same pace. Real attention does not behave that way. The same buyer reads a peer recommendation, a search result, and a long-form explainer in different states of mind, and each surface has a different job to do.

Mapping attention this way lets a team see:

- Where attention is most likely to be earned, and where it is most likely to be lost.
- Which touchpoint is doing real persuasion work, and which is only assumed to.
- Which channels deserve a content asset, and which are noise for this Mindset.
- Where a single weak step quietly breaks an otherwise strong journey.

## Where the Attention Journey sits in the system

The Attention Journey is the bridge between understanding a buyer and producing content for them. It connects the early and later parts of the Creative Engines flow:

1. [Mindset Intelligence](./mindset-intelligence.md) establishes who the buyer is and why they act.
2. The Attention Journey maps where that buyer's attention travels and what each touchpoint must accomplish.
3. [Go-To-Market Strategy Planning](../product/brand-strategy/go-to-market-strategy-planning.md) decides what the brand should say at each point of the journey.
4. [Creative Studio](../product/creative-studio/README.md) produces the specific asset each touchpoint needs.
5. [Performance & Accountability](../product/performance-accountability/README.md) feeds back what actually earned or lost attention, and the map updates.

Because the same attention map feeds both strategy and production, a content asset can be made for a precise point in the journey rather than for a vague audience.

## Public boundary

This page explains the Attention Journey as a product concept. It does not publish how Creative Engines selects, weights, scores, or sequences channels and touchpoints, which data sources inform the map, or any internal model behind it.
