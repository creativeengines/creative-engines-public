# Brand Preview

Brand Preview is the public concept for giving a buyer, partner, or prospect a structured first read on a brand through the Creative Engines lens.

The public website presents Brand Preview as a way to see a brand through its customers' eyes. In repository language, it is a buyer handoff artifact: a compact, structured way to explain how Creative Engines reads brand context, audience mindsets, strategic narratives, and content opportunities.

## What it is for

- Helping a prospect understand the product before a deeper conversation.
- Giving an agency a structured way to discuss a client or prospect brand.
- Turning a public website or brand surface into a more useful strategy discussion.
- Showing the type of thinking Creative Engines organizes without exposing private methodology.

## What it may contain

A Brand Preview may summarize public-facing themes such as:

- Brand context and positioning signals.
- Audience segments worth exploring.
- Public-safe mindset summaries.
- Strategic narrative directions.
- Examples of how content could become more specific and less generic.

## Why it matters

High-fit buyers often need to trust the thinking before they trust the platform. Brand Preview is a product-led education surface: it helps a reader understand the shape of Creative Engines without forcing them to inspect internal systems.

A Brand Preview is the visible output of real analysis, not a template. It reflects how Creative Engines reads a brand through buyer [Mindsets](./mindset-intelligence.md) and the [Attention Journey](./attention-journey.md), which is why it can show specific strategic directions rather than generic observations.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public concept. It does not publish qualification logic, automation details, private source handling, internal templates, or delivery operations.
