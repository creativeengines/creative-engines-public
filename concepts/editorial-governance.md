# Editorial Governance

Editorial governance is the operating layer that keeps AI-assisted content aligned with strategy, audience understanding, brand context, and quality standards.

Creative Engines is not built around the idea that more output is automatically better. It is built around the idea that content should pass through clear editorial judgment before it becomes public.

## What does editorial governance protect?

- Brand voice and category position.
- Audience relevance.
- Claim quality.
- Strategic focus.
- Reuse of approved brand context.
- Human accountability.

## Why it matters

AI can increase production speed. Without governance, speed often produces low-context material that looks acceptable but does not teach, persuade, or compound.

Creative Engines treats AI as something professionals pilot. Editorial governance is the cockpit: it gives teams the context and review structure needed to use AI without giving up judgment.

The public website expresses this through three recurring ideas:

- Portfolio control: teams need to keep different brands distinct.
- Traceable intelligence: content should have a visible reason behind it.
- Human quality gates: AI suggestions should pass through editorial judgment before they become public.

Those ideas make Creative Engines different from a pure drafting surface. The work is not finished when text is generated. It is finished when the asset is aligned, useful, reviewed, and ready to represent the brand.

## What is Brand Guardian?

Brand Guardian is the public name for the review posture Creative Engines applies to brand and editorial work. At a client-facing level, it means content should be checked for:

- Brand safety.
- Narrative discipline.
- Voice consistency.
- Distinctive assets.
- Misinterpretation risk.
- Offer clarity.
- Fact-checking.

These checks are not meant to replace the editor, strategist, or brand owner. They make review more consistent by giving human judgment a clearer structure.

The target is a better human, not a flawless machine. The work aims for eight out of ten that a person sharpens the last stretch to ten, because perfect automation removes the person's fingerprints – and with them the ownership and accountability that make the work theirs.

## Public boundary

This page explains the public concept. It does not describe private review systems or protected assessment rules.
