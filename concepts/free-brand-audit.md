# Free Brand Audit

The free brand audit is the public way into Creative Engines. A brand's public website goes in, and a structured first read of that brand and its buyers comes back by email. There is nothing to install and no call to book first.

It works on any brand a reader can point at – their own, or a client's – which is why it fits an agency's daily act as naturally as an in-house team's.

The artifact that arrives keeps its own name in conversation: the **Preview Deck**. The audit is the action; the Preview Deck is the thing delivered.

## What it is for

- Letting a prospect judge the thinking before trusting the platform.
- Giving an agency a structured way to open a conversation about a client or a prospect brand.
- Turning a public website into a more useful strategy discussion than a capabilities pitch.
- Showing the shape of the work on real material, without anyone inspecting internal systems.

## What it may cover

A first read may summarize public-facing themes such as:

- What the brand's public surface currently signals to a buyer.
- Buyer perspectives worth taking seriously, as evidence-grounded models rather than real respondents.
- Candidate directions worth testing next – never a predicted winner.

## Why it matters

High-fit buyers need to trust the thinking before they trust the platform, and the fastest honest way to judge a marketing tool is to point it at real material and see whether the first output is specific. That is what this is for.

The read is the visible output of real analysis rather than a template. It reflects how Creative Engines reads a brand through buyer [Mindsets](./mindset-intelligence.md) and the [Attention Journey](./attention-journey.md), which is why it can name specific directions instead of generic observations.

It is a first read, not a strategy engagement, and it makes no promise about what it will find.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public entry point. It does not publish qualification logic, automation details, private source handling, internal templates, scoring, or delivery operations.
