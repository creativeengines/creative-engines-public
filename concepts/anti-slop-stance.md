# Anti-Slop Stance

Creative Engines is built against low-effort AI marketing.

The product stance is simple: quality matters more than quantity. Content should be grounded, reviewed, and connected to strategy. Publishing more weak content is not a marketing advantage.

## What Creative Engines rejects

- Generic output that could belong to any brand.
- Volume as a substitute for quality.
- One-click marketing claims.
- Content that ignores buyer belief.
- Content production with no editorial accountability.

## What Creative Engines supports

- Clear market focus.
- Buyer understanding.
- Brand-owned context.
- Human review.
- Reusable content assets.
- Learning from what happens after publication.

## Why it matters

AI has made low-context content easier to produce. That makes editorial quality and strategic clarity more important, not less.

Creative Engines is best understood as an editorial system, not a content factory.

The website frames the enemy plainly: generic AI output that sounds like everyone else. Creative Engines exists for teams that want AI-assisted speed without surrendering strategy, voice, or buyer relevance.

## Public signals of low-effort AI content

- The asset could belong to almost any brand in the category.
- The message tries to please every audience at once.
- The draft follows the immediate instruction but ignores the strategic situation.
- The content has no clear buyer belief, proof need, or editorial reason to exist.
- Review focuses only on polish, not on whether the piece should be published.

## Creative tactics and genericity

Creative Engines treats AI slop as more than weak prose. Generic content often comes from generic creative moves: familiar hooks, predictable structures, overused metaphors, vague claims, and safe category language.

The product uses a 200+ creative tactics catalog and review habits to push work toward more specific, brand-owned ideas. Publicly describable influences include bisociation, distinctive assets, creative effectiveness research, and structured creative problem solving.

The point is not to force every asset to be unusual. The point is to prevent AI-assisted work from defaulting to the easiest pattern.
