# Market Intelligence

Market Intelligence is the product area for understanding the outside context around a brand: category language, competitor positioning, public signals, buyer conversations, and shifts in how the market explains the problem.

In a demo, this is the part of Creative Engines that prevents a team from planning content in a vacuum.

## What it helps users do

Market Intelligence helps users ask:

- What is the category already saying?
- Which claims are becoming generic or overused?
- Which buyer problems are being under-explained?
- Which competitor narratives are shaping expectations?
- Which market signals should influence strategy before content is produced?

The output should help the team understand the strategic environment, not merely collect links or summarize competitors.

## What it helps decide

Market Intelligence informs decisions such as:

- Which narratives are worth defending.
- Which category assumptions should be challenged.
- Which buyer objections need better explanation.
- Which content themes are crowded and which are still open.
- Where the brand should sound different from generic AI-generated category language.

## How it connects to other product areas

Market context feeds [Brand Strategy](../brand-strategy/README.md), because positioning and go-to-market planning should reflect what the market is already hearing.

It also feeds [Mindset Intelligence](../mindset-intelligence/README.md), because buyer beliefs are shaped by the claims, stories, failures, and expectations they encounter before they meet the brand.

When the team moves into [Creative Studio](../creative-studio/README.md), market context helps content avoid saying what every other brand in the category already says.

## Public boundary

This page explains the public product purpose. It does not publish source-selection rules, monitoring mechanics, internal research procedures, scoring models, or protected market-analysis methods.
