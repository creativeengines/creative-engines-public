# Brand Strategy

Brand Strategy is the product area for deciding what the brand stands for, who it serves, what it should say, and how the strategy should guide content work.

In a demo, this is where Creative Engines moves from raw brand context into usable strategic direction.

## What users can do

Brand Strategy helps users work with:

- Brand DNA: positioning, voice, values, content requirements, and strategic context.
- Offerings: the products, services, or offers that need to be explained.
- Strategic narratives: the storylines that connect buyer Mindsets to the brand's point of view.
- Go-to-market strategy planning: the connection between Mindsets, offerings, narratives, buying stages, and content direction.
- Reviewable strategy artifacts that can guide content production and team alignment.

This area is meant to make strategy usable inside the product, not leave it trapped in a document or a senior person's memory.

## What it helps decide

Brand Strategy helps a team decide:

- Which buyers the brand is trying to reach.
- What those buyers need to understand or believe.
- Which offering or message should be connected to each buyer state.
- Which narrative should lead the content.
- Which go-to-market direction should become actual content work.
- What should be approved before production starts.

Creative Engines treats Go-To-Market strategy planning as a practical bridge between audience understanding and content execution. The planning surface is meant to help a team see the relationship between buyers, narratives, offers, and content priorities in one place.

## How it connects to other product areas

Brand Strategy depends on [Brand Knowledge](../brand-knowledge/README.md) for approved source context and on [Mindset Intelligence](../mindset-intelligence/README.md) for buyer understanding.

It feeds [Creative Studio](../creative-studio/README.md), where strategy becomes content, and [Content Distribution](../content-distribution/README.md), where strategy becomes planned work across campaigns and tasks.

## Public boundary

This page explains the user-facing role of Brand Strategy. It does not publish protected strategy-generation methods, internal planning logic, private prioritization models, or reproducible methodology details.
