# Go-To-Market Strategy Planning

Go-To-Market Strategy Planning is the capability that connects buyer understanding, brand strategy, offerings, narratives, buying stages, and content direction.

In a client demo, this is the moment where Creative Engines stops being a content tool and becomes a strategy-to-execution system.

## What the user sees

A user should be able to review the strategic ingredients of a brand in one place:

- Target buyer Mindsets.
- Offerings the brand wants to explain or sell.
- Strategic narratives that frame the buyer's problem and the brand's point of view.
- Buying-stage logic that explains what the buyer needs before action.
- Content directions that can become campaigns, tasks, or assets.

The value is in seeing how these pieces relate. The user should not have to rebuild the strategy manually every time a content task is created.

## What it helps the team do

Go-To-Market Strategy Planning helps a team:

- Turn buyer understanding into a practical plan.
- Connect each major content direction to a Mindset and narrative.
- See which offers need better explanation.
- Reduce strategy handoff drift before production starts.
- Move from strategic discussion to content work with more continuity.

This is especially useful for teams where a senior strategist understands the market but the production team receives only a simplified brief.

## What it helps decide

The planning area helps answer:

- Which buyer Mindsets should the strategy prioritize?
- Which narrative should be used for each buyer state?
- Which offerings need education, contrast, trust-building, or urgency?
- What should content do at each stage of the buyer journey?
- Which work should move into Creative Studio or Content Distribution next?

## How it connects to the product

This capability depends on [Mindset Intelligence](../mindset-intelligence/README.md), [Brand Knowledge](../brand-knowledge/README.md), and [Brand Strategy](./README.md).

It feeds [Creative Studio](../creative-studio/README.md) by giving content work a strategic source. It feeds [Content Distribution](../content-distribution/README.md) by helping campaigns and tasks stay aligned with the plan.
