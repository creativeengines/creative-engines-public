# Buyer Mindset Workspace

The Buyer Mindset Workspace is where a team reviews, edits, and uses the rational-emotional portraits that guide Creative Engines content work.

Mindsets are more specific than personas. They describe the buyer's job, desired outcome, pressures, doubts, current workarounds, emotional frame, and readiness to move.

## What the user sees

A user should be able to inspect a set of Mindsets for a brand and understand:

- Who the Mindset represents.
- What job the buyer is trying to get done.
- What is pushing them away from the current situation.
- What is pulling them toward a better state.
- What anxiety or habit may prevent action.
- What outcome the buyer wants.
- How this Mindset should influence content and strategy.

The workspace is meant to make buyer understanding usable during daily work, not hide it in research notes.

## Source context

Mindsets can be informed by first-party material such as reviews, comments, customer conversations, support notes, and other user-generated context. They can also be developed through Creative Engines research methodology when a team needs a structured view of buyer belief before it has enough direct material.

The user-facing value is not the source material itself. The value is a usable buyer lens that can shape strategy, content, review, and learning.

## What it helps the team do

The Buyer Mindset Workspace helps teams:

- Move beyond generic audience labels.
- Compare different buyer states inside the same market.
- Keep content attached to a specific buyer problem.
- Review and refine audience assumptions before production.
- Give strategists, writers, and reviewers a shared buyer lens.

The result should be content that feels more specific because it is aimed at a real state of mind.

## What it helps decide

The workspace helps answer:

- Which Mindset is this asset for?
- What belief does this buyer need to change?
- What resistance should the content address?
- What language would feel credible to this buyer?
- Which strategic narrative should be connected to this Mindset?

## How it connects to the product

Mindsets feed [Go-To-Market Strategy Planning](../brand-strategy/go-to-market-strategy-planning.md), [Creative Studio](../creative-studio/README.md), and [Content Campaigns and Tasks](../content-distribution/content-campaigns-and-tasks.md).

The same Mindsets also power two distinctive steps: they anchor the [Attention Journey](../../concepts/attention-journey.md) that maps where a buyer's attention travels, and they make up the audience focus group that reviews content before release, described in the [Multi-Agent Creative Department](../../concepts/multi-agent-creative-department.md).

They also connect to the public [Segments](../../segments/README.md) library, where this repository explains who Creative Engines is for and why different buyer groups need it.

## Public boundary

This page explains the workspace and its user-facing role. It does not publish how Mindsets are generated, validated, ranked, selected, or improved internally.
