# Mindset Intelligence

Mindset Intelligence is the product area for understanding buyer beliefs, anxieties, motivations, workarounds, and decision states.

It is one of the core ideas behind Creative Engines: content should be written for the way a buyer is thinking and feeling about a problem, not only for a job title or demographic label.

## What users can do

In a product walkthrough, Mindset Intelligence is explained as the workspace where a team can:

- Generate or review buyer Mindsets for a brand.
- Read the buyer's core job, desired outcome, tensions, objections, and emotional frame.
- Compare different Mindsets inside the same segment.
- Edit or validate Mindsets before using them in downstream work.
- Attach content decisions to the Mindset the asset is meant to serve.

The point is not to produce decorative personas. The point is to make audience understanding operational.

## What it helps decide

Mindset Intelligence helps teams decide:

- Which buyer state a piece of content should address.
- What the buyer already believes.
- What the buyer is trying to escape.
- What the buyer is pulled toward.
- What fear, habit, or skepticism may block action.
- Which content language will feel specific instead of generic.

This is why Mindsets are connected to strategy, narratives, and content production. The Mindset gives the work a target state of mind.

## What a Mindset contains

A Mindset is a deep rational-emotional portrait, not a decorative persona. For each one the module works with the buyer's core job and desired outcome; the four switching forces (push, pull, anxiety, habit); a behavioral reading of capability, opportunity, and motivation; demographics, professional profile, and psychographics; an emotional palette drawn from a model of 13 states; sensitivity indicators for pain, purchasing power, and price, time, quality, and variety, plus a fear-to-aspiration axis; the buyer's activation channels and current solutions; and their standing beliefs, objections, and workarounds. The methods behind these readings are described at a safe level in [The Methodology Base](../../concepts/methodology-base.md).

## What the module produces today

- A **library of Mindset profiles** for a brand, each with the full profile above and a generated **persona** - a name, a face, and a short description.
- **Strategic narratives** attached to each Mindset.
- A public **Brand Preview Deck**: a prospect drops in a company URL, and the module generates the brand's Mindsets, contrasts two of them, and assembles a sample narrative and sample content into a shareable deck. See [Brand Preview](../../concepts/brand-preview.md).
- An audience focus group built from the same Mindsets, which can react to content before release - what was understood, felt, and found unclear. See the [Multi-Agent Creative Department](../../concepts/multi-agent-creative-department.md).

## Where the module is headed

Mindset Intelligence is also the foundation for the approaches Creative Engines is building out:

- The [Attention Journey](../../concepts/attention-journey.md): mapping where each Mindset's attention travels across touchpoints and media channels, to plan strategy and production.
- Prioritizing Mindsets into tiers - the ideal customers, those to nurture, the opportunistic, and an explicit negative tier the brand should not write for.

## How it connects to other product areas

Mindset Intelligence supports [Brand Strategy](../brand-strategy/README.md) by clarifying who the brand is trying to move and what those buyers need to believe.

It supports [Creative Studio](../creative-studio/README.md) by giving the content system a concrete audience lens before writing starts, and by supplying the focus group that reviews finished work.

It supports [Content Distribution](../content-distribution/README.md) by helping campaigns and tasks stay attached to a buyer state rather than a vague audience label.

## Public boundary

This page explains what Mindset Intelligence does for users. It does not publish how Mindsets are generated, evaluated, scored, selected, or improved internally.
