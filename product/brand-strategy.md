# Brand Strategy

Brand Strategy is the product area for deciding what a brand stands for, who it serves, what it should say, and how that strategy should guide content work.

This is where Creative Engines moves from raw brand context into usable strategic direction – and where a senior strategist's understanding stops living only in their head or a slide, and becomes something production can follow.

## How strategy comes together

1. **Brand DNA** – point the system at a brand's website (or documents) and it drafts the foundational profile: value proposition, brand voice, positioning, core values, content requirements, and an initial offerings list.
2. **Offerings and narratives** – maintain what the brand sells, and generate the per-Mindset story the brand tells for each audience.
3. **Go-to-market blueprint** – assemble one navigable view linking the buyer-journey phases against Mindsets, offerings, channels, and narratives.
4. **Approve** – the strategy owner approves the blueprint before content production starts; a change to an upstream piece propagates down.

## What it does today

- **Brand creation** sets up a workspace that scopes all knowledge, Mindsets, and content to one brand.
- **Brand DNA extraction from a website** drafts the brand's foundational profile in minutes rather than a blank-page workshop; the profile is viewable, inline-editable, and exportable as a PDF.
- **Offerings** maintain the brand's catalog of products and services, so content and strategy tie back to what the brand actually sells.
- **Strategic narratives** generate the per-Mindset story – the tension it pushes against, the change it offers, a single-minded proposition, the customer transformation, and candidate taglines – so every audience gets its own coherent story rather than one generic line stretched across everyone.
- **Personas** add a targeting lever inside the content brief.
- **An AI-visibility and SEO/GEO audit** measures how visible a brand is to AI assistants and in search and returns a branded report – a concrete read on where the brand stands, and a sales-ready artifact.
- **A content grid** lays out recurring content themes by channel, format, and weekly cadence – the planning scaffold the calendar draws on.
- **A go-to-market blueprint** links the buyer-journey phases against Mindsets, offerings, channels, and narratives in one approved view, so no content begins from an unapproved plan.
- **Buyer-journey stages** give strategy and content a shared phase vocabulary.

## On the roadmap

- **A no-website Brand DNA path** that derives the same brand foundation from uploaded internal documents when there is no usable public site.

## What it helps decide

- Which buyers the brand is trying to reach, and what they need to understand or believe.
- Which offering or message connects to each buyer state.
- Which narrative should lead the content.
- Which go-to-market direction should become actual content work.
- What should be approved before production starts.

## How it connects to other product areas

Brand Strategy depends on [Brand Knowledge](./brand-knowledge.md) for approved source context and on [Mindset Intelligence](./mindset-intelligence.md) for buyer understanding. It feeds [Creative Studio](./creative-studio.md), where strategy becomes content, and [Content Distribution](./content-distribution.md), where strategy becomes planned work across pillars, campaigns, and tasks. Keeping these connected is how the product reduces strategy handoff drift – the loss of context when a plan moves from a senior strategist to a simplified production brief.

## Public boundary

This page explains the user-facing role of Brand Strategy. It does not publish protected strategy-generation methods, internal planning logic, private prioritization models, or reproducible methodology details.
