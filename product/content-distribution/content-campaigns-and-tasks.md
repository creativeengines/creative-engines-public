# Content Campaigns and Tasks

Content Campaigns and Tasks are the product concepts that help a team turn strategy-grounded assets into managed work.

Creative Engines treats content as part of an operating system. A post, article, carousel, page, or comment should be connected to a campaign purpose, buyer Mindset, strategic narrative, review state, and intended use.

## What the user sees

A user should be able to understand content work through structures such as:

- Campaigns that group related content around a strategic objective.
- Tasks that describe what needs to be created, reviewed, reused, or published.
- Calendar views that show planned work over time.
- Channel planning that keeps output connected to where the audience will encounter it.
- Review states that show what is planned, drafted, approved, or already used.

This helps teams avoid treating every asset as a disconnected one-off.

## What it helps the team do

Content Campaigns and Tasks help teams:

- Translate strategy into actual work.
- Keep content production organized across formats.
- Connect each task to a buyer Mindset and strategic reason.
- Coordinate review before publication.
- Reuse strong assets instead of letting them disappear.
- See what work exists, what is missing, and what is ready.

## What it helps decide

This capability helps answer:

- What content work belongs to this campaign?
- What should be created next?
- Which Mindset and narrative should guide the task?
- Which channel or format is appropriate?
- What review state is the asset in?
- What should be reused or improved?

## How it connects to the product

Content Campaigns and Tasks receive assets from [Creative Studio](../creative-studio/README.md) and strategic direction from [Go-To-Market Strategy Planning](../brand-strategy/go-to-market-strategy-planning.md).

They support [Performance & Accountability](../performance-accountability/README.md) by making future learning traceable to the work that produced it.
