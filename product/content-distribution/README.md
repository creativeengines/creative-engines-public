# Content Distribution

Content Distribution is the product area for turning content from isolated drafts into managed work: campaigns, tasks, calendars, channels, review states, and reuse.

This is where Creative Engines connects production to an operating rhythm.

## What users can do

Content Distribution helps users organize:

- Content campaigns around a strategic purpose.
- Content tasks connected to brand context, buyer Mindsets, narratives, and offers.
- Calendar views for upcoming production or publication work.
- Channel planning at a business level.
- Review states such as planned, drafted, approved, reused, or published.
- Reusable content assets that should continue supporting the strategy after the first draft.

The product area matters because content strategy often fails after the draft is created. If the work is not planned, reviewed, scheduled, reused, or connected to a campaign, it becomes disconnected activity.

## What it helps decide

Content Distribution helps teams decide:

- Which content work belongs together.
- Which audience or Mindset each task serves.
- Which strategic narrative the task should support.
- Which channel or format makes sense for the asset.
- What should happen before a draft is considered ready.
- How a piece should be reused instead of forgotten.

## How it connects to other product areas

Content Distribution receives strategy from [Brand Strategy](../brand-strategy/README.md) and assets from [Creative Studio](../creative-studio/README.md).

It gives [Performance & Accountability](../performance-accountability/README.md) a clearer structure for learning, because outcomes can be tied back to campaigns, tasks, buyer states, and strategic intent.

## Public boundary
