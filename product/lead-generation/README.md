# Lead Generation

Lead Generation is the product area for public-facing entry points and handoff artifacts that help a buyer, partner, or agency understand whether Creative Engines is relevant.

This area is not only about capturing contact information. It is about giving a prospect a structured first read that demonstrates how Creative Engines thinks about a brand, its buyers, and its content opportunities.

## What users can do

Lead Generation helps users and partners:

- Create public entry points that collect basic brand and contact context.
- Generate structured first-read artifacts for prospects.
- Hand off a prospect from a public form or conversation into a more informed product discussion.
- Help agencies or partners show a client how Creative Engines reads a brand.
- Connect a lead's public brand surface to brand context, Mindsets, narratives, and sample content directions.

The practical purpose is to make the first conversation better. A buyer should not need to start from a blank call; they can arrive with a structured artifact that gives the discussion more substance.

## What it helps decide

Lead Generation helps teams decide:

- Whether a prospect's situation is worth a deeper product conversation.
- Which brand context should be prepared before the call.
- Which buyer Mindsets and narratives may be worth discussing.
- Which public materials should be shared with an AI-fluent prospect, partner, or investor.
- How to move from interest to a concrete product review.

## How it connects to other product areas

Lead Generation consumes [Brand Strategy](../brand-strategy/README.md), [Mindset Intelligence](../mindset-intelligence/README.md), and [Creative Studio](../creative-studio/README.md) outputs to create useful public-facing handoff material.

It connects naturally to this public repository, because AI-fluent readers may use the repository itself as a structured evaluation surface before a deeper conversation.
