# Creative Engines

**Creative Engines is a strategy-to-content marketing platform. A team grounds its strategy in evidence anyone can open, tests a message before publishing it, and publishes content that holds the approved direction.**

It is built for marketing teams, agencies, and founders – people who already know what good work looks like and need it to survive contact with volume. This repository is the public, structured place to understand how that works. It is written for both people and AI assistants: an AI tool pointed at it can answer questions about the product, the approach, who it is for, and how it differs from generic AI content tools.

## The problem it exists against

A brand's approved strategy lives in decks and in people's heads. Every handoff – a new team member, a freelancer, an AI chat – rebuilds it from memory, so what gets published drifts away from what was agreed.

Each piece looks defensible on its own. Put them in front of the same buyer over a few weeks and they pull against each other: the awareness post says one thing, the landing page another, an outside contributor runs a third angle. Nothing accumulates, and the spend never compounds.

AI did not cause this. AI made production cheap, which means a handoff that used to cost a week now costs a morning – at ten times the volume, with the same reasoning missing from each copy. The bottleneck moved from making content to knowing what to say, and the drift moved with it.

Full explanation: [Strategy handoff drift](./concepts/strategy-handoff-drift.md).

## How the work goes: Ground → Test → Publish

The Creative Engines website states the plan in three steps, because three is what a first screen can carry. This repository is where each step opens up. The three names come from the website; the depth underneath them is what this repository adds.

**Ground.** Build the strategy on the brand's own material and on real buyer evidence, with sources a reader can open. What the brand already knows becomes approved, searchable context; the market around it is read for what buyers actually say; and buyers are modelled by what they believe, fear, and need to resolve rather than by demographic labels.

- [Brand Knowledge](./product/brand-knowledge.md) · [Market Intelligence](./product/market-intelligence.md) · [Mindset Intelligence](./product/mindset-intelligence.md) · [Brand Strategy](./product/brand-strategy.md)
- Concepts: [Mindset Intelligence](./concepts/mindset-intelligence.md) · [Attention Journey](./concepts/attention-journey.md) · [The Methodology Base](./concepts/methodology-base.md)

**Test.** Put a candidate message in front of **synthetic Mindsets** – buyer models built from that same evidence, never real respondents. Each reaction carries a **Believability** reading: how convincing that Mindset found the piece. The reading is comparative, and it exists to help a team choose between candidate messages. It is never a prediction of response, conversion, or revenue. A person approves what survived.

- [Test before you publish](./concepts/test-before-you-publish.md) · [Multi-Agent Creative Department](./concepts/multi-agent-creative-department.md) · [Anti-slop stance](./concepts/anti-slop-stance.md)

**Publish.** Turn the approved direction into content that sounds like the brand, reviewed by a person before it represents them. Approved narratives and content pillars govern what gets made, a governed production process writes and challenges it, and distribution carries approved work to channels on a rhythm – with a human gate before anything goes out.

- [Creative Studio](./product/creative-studio.md) · [Content Distribution](./product/content-distribution.md)
- Concepts: [Editorial governance](./concepts/editorial-governance.md) · [Brand voice](./concepts/brand-voice.md) · [A brand voice that survives AI](./concepts/voice-at-ai-scale.md) · [Strategy-grounded content](./concepts/strategy-grounded-content.md)

**Then it loops.** Live results feed back into the next decision, so each round is better-calibrated than the last.

- [Performance & Accountability](./product/performance-accountability.md) · [The value that compounds](./concepts/compounding-value.md)

The connected, end-to-end view is the [Strategy-to-Content Pipeline](./concepts/strategy-to-content-pipeline.md).

## The core idea: a Reason to Buy

The spine of Creative Engines is the [Reason to Buy](./concepts/reason-to-buy.md) – the reason a brand's buyers choose it, rather than the interchangeable mass running the same playbook. It is the customer-facing name for a value proposition, and on the website it is said in plain words: find why buyers choose you, and put it behind every message.

Everything else serves it. [Mindset Intelligence](./concepts/mindset-intelligence.md) grounds the reason in the buyers' own words, the [pre-publication test](./concepts/test-before-you-publish.md) puts it under pressure before the spend, governance carries the version that held up into everything the brand ships, and a [performance loop](./concepts/compounding-value.md) reports how it actually performed.

Creative Engines improves the odds. It does not guarantee a financial result, and the marketer's judgment stays in charge.

## Product stance

- Quality beats quantity.
- Strategy beats generic generation.
- Editorial control matters.
- Buyer beliefs matter more than demographic labels.
- AI should be piloted by professionals, not treated as a magic button.
- The product amplifies people; it does not replace them. Creative Engines exists to make the professionals who use it sharper – better creative strategists, not sidelined operators. The mechanisms are only as good as the expert pilot directing them, and without that human they have no point.
- Creative Engines scales a marketing team's capacity without losing context or quality, and keeps the team's strategy and judgment in charge.
- Content should become a reusable business asset, not disposable feed filler.
