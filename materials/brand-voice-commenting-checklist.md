# Brand Voice Commenting Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing short public comments, replies, or social contributions that should sound like a brand's own thinking.

## Before writing

- The target conversation is relevant to the brand's audience.
- The comment has a point of view, not only agreement.
- The brand's approved language and avoided language are available.
- The comment can be connected to a known narrative, tactic, belief, or insight.

## During review

- The comment sounds like the brand, not a generic assistant.
- It adds something specific to the conversation.
- It avoids empty praise, vague agreement, and over-polished filler.
- It does not make unsupported claims.
- A human can approve it quickly without rewriting the whole thing.

## After publishing

- Useful comments can become source material for future content ideas.
- Repeated audience reactions can inform future positioning and content direction.
- The team can distinguish meaningful engagement from vanity activity.
