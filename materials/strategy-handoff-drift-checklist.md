# Strategy Handoff Drift Checklist

Use this checklist to review whether strategy is surviving the move from approved thinking to actual content work.

## Handoff checks

- The task includes the reason the asset should exist.
- The buyer situation is described in plain language.
- The intended audience mindset is clear enough to guide choices.
- The brand voice and point of view are available to the producer.
- The reviewer can see the strategic source behind the work.
- The asset is not judged only by whether it follows the brief.

## Drift warning signs

- The team keeps asking a senior person to explain the same context.
- Drafts are polished but strategically weak.
- Feedback repeatedly says "this does not sound like the client."
- The brief says what to create but not why it matters.
- Production tickets contain only a small fraction of the original strategic context.
- Review work becomes rescue work.

## Better handoff questions

- What should the reader believe after this?
- What buyer hesitation is this asset meant to reduce?
- What approved brand context should constrain the draft?
- What would make this content feel interchangeable?
- What should a human editor check before publication?
