# AI-Assisted Content Governance Checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate whether AI-assisted content work has enough governance to protect quality.

## Before production

- The target segment is defined.
- The buyer belief or hesitation is stated.
- The brand context is available.
- The strategic narrative or purpose is explicit.
- The intended channel and format are clear.
- The asset has a review owner.

## During production

- Drafts are checked against the brand context.
- Claims are reviewed before publication.
- Generic phrasing is removed.
- The work stays tied to the buyer situation.
- The reviewer can explain why the asset should exist.
- The final version still sounds like the brand's own thinking.

## After publication

- The asset is saved as part of a reusable library.
- Useful learning is captured.
- Future content decisions can refer back to the asset.
- The team can identify whether the content supported its intended purpose.
