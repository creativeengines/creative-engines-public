# Editorial Quality Checklist

Use this checklist to review whether an AI-assisted content asset is ready for serious use.

## Checklist

- The audience is clear.
- The buyer belief or concern is clear.
- The asset has a specific purpose.
- The brand voice is recognizable.
- Claims are safe and supportable.
- The content does not sound interchangeable with another brand.
- The content carries a point of view, not only a polished answer.
- The structure helps the reader understand the point quickly.
- The piece teaches, persuades, or clarifies.
- A human editor has reviewed it.
- The asset can be reused or referenced later.

## Warning signs

- The content is correct but generic.
- The piece uses broad claims without proof.
- The audience could be anyone.
- The conclusion does not change what the reader understands.
- The asset exists only to fill a channel.
