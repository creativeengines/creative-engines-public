# F500 Regulated Finance and Insurance Brand: Buying Committee

## Titles commonly involved

- Brand Director
- Compliance-Constrained CMO
- VP Digital / Omnisurface Marketing
- Promotional or Marketing Review Lead
- Marketing Procurement Lead
- CFO / VP Finance
- CIO / Head of Regulated Data Governance
- VP Marketing Operations

## Decision-process summary

The decision is trust-led. Marketing needs the system, but governance, data, review, and operational leaders must be confident that it reduces ambiguity rather than creating new risk.

The exact path depends on company structure, but the pattern is consistent: one team feels the pain first, several stakeholders need confidence, and the system must be understood as strategy and governance support rather than a shortcut around human judgment.
