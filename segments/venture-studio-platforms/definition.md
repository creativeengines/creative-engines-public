# Venture Studio Platform Group: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Venture studios and platform groups that support multiple portfolio companies with shared go-to-market, brand, and content capabilities.

## Boundaries

- Organization with multiple ventures, portfolio companies, founders, operators, and shared platform resources.
- The team needs repeatable strategy, messaging, buyer research, and content support across many company contexts.
- Each company must sound distinct while benefiting from a common operating layer.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from agencies because the work supports portfolio company creation and acceleration rather than outside client service alone.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
