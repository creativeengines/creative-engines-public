# Online Microbusiness: Account Profile

## Operating context

- Tiny online-first business led by one founder or a very small team.
- The business may have a real niche and repeatable offer, but little formal marketing infrastructure.
- Content, landing pages, email, social, and offer education are often handled by the same person.

## What this means for content

For this segment, content is not only a writing task. It is a coordination problem. The team needs approved context, buyer understanding, review discipline, and a shared way to decide what should be said.

## Decision environment

The owner decides directly and only adopts systems that feel lightweight, useful, and immediately understandable.
