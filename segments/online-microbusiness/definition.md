# Online Microbusiness: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Small online-first businesses that sell products, courses, services, newsletters, communities, or niche offers and need clearer positioning and reusable content discipline.

## Boundaries

- Tiny online-first business led by one founder or a very small team.
- The business may have a real niche and repeatable offer, but little formal marketing infrastructure.
- Content, landing pages, email, social, and offer education are often handled by the same person.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from established small businesses because the operating structure is smaller and more founder-dependent; different from local microbusinesses because digital content is central to demand.

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
