# Solo Service Practice: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Independent consultants, fractional leaders, niche specialists, expert advisors, and solo operators who sell expertise and need better leverage without diluting their method.

## Boundaries

- One-person or very small expert practice where reputation, method, client trust, and repeatable delivery matter.
- The operator may serve several clients at once and carry too much strategy in their head.
- They need content, briefs, audits, playbooks, and client-facing material that sounds like their expertise, not generic AI.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from solo personal brands because the buyer pays for a service outcome, not mainly for audience attention or creator identity.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
