# Solo Service Practice: Account Profile

## Operating context

- One-person or very small expert practice where reputation, method, client trust, and repeatable delivery matter.
- The operator may serve several clients at once and carry too much strategy in their head.
- They need content, briefs, audits, playbooks, and client-facing material that sounds like their expertise, not generic AI.
- The practice often has strong judgment but weak operational packaging.

## What this means for content

For this segment, content is not only a writing task. It is a coordination problem. The team needs approved context, buyer understanding, review discipline, and a shared way to decide what should be said.

## Decision environment

The operator decides directly, usually after seeing whether the system can preserve their judgment and help them serve clients with less repetitive rewriting.
