# Boutique Creative Agency: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Small creative or strategy-led agencies where founder judgment, taste, client context, and senior handoff determine the quality of work.

## Boundaries

- Small agency with founder, strategy, creative, account, and production responsibilities often blended across a lean team.
- The agency wins through taste, trust, speed, or specialization, but struggles to scale the founder or senior strategist.
- AI can help with leverage, but only if it preserves the strategic and creative judgment clients pay for.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from solo service practices because the boutique has a team and client delivery system; different from mid-tier agencies because senior founder context still dominates the operating model.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
