# Boutique Creative Agency: Account Profile

## Operating context

- Small agency with founder, strategy, creative, account, and production responsibilities often blended across a lean team.
- The agency wins through taste, trust, speed, or specialization, but struggles to scale the founder or senior strategist.
- AI can help with leverage, but only if it preserves the strategic and creative judgment clients pay for.
- Client briefs often carry the what while the team still needs the why.

## What this means for content

For this segment, content is not only a writing task. It is a coordination problem. The team needs approved context, buyer understanding, review discipline, and a shared way to decide what should be said.

## Decision environment

The founder or senior strategic leader decides when the system feels like it protects the agency method and reduces handoff drift rather than replacing judgment.
