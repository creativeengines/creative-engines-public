# Mid-Market B2B SaaS Company: Buying Committee

## Titles commonly involved

- VP Marketing
- Product Marketing Lead
- Content Director
- VP Sales / CRO
- RevOps Administrator
- VP Engineering / CTO
- Founder / CEO
- Marketing Operations Lead

## Decision-process summary

Marketing usually champions the need, but sales, product, RevOps, and technical stakeholders influence whether the system is trusted enough to enter core go-to-market work.

The exact path depends on company structure, but the pattern is consistent: one team feels the pain first, several stakeholders need confidence, and the system must be understood as strategy and governance support rather than a shortcut around human judgment.
