# Mid-Market B2B SaaS Company: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

B2B SaaS companies with real go-to-market motion, multiple buyer stakeholders, and pressure to produce clear, differentiated, defensible content in a crowded category.

## Boundaries

- Product-led, sales-led, or hybrid SaaS company with marketing, sales, product marketing, RevOps, engineering, and leadership stakeholders.
- The team has enough content volume to feel the cost of inconsistency and enough category pressure to fear sounding generic.
- AI adoption is common, but the company still needs defensible positioning, buyer-specific messaging, and rigorous content judgment.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from startups because the go-to-market system already exists; different from service businesses because the product is software and the content must support product, sales, demand, and customer education together.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
