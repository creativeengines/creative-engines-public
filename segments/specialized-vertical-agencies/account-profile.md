# Specialized Vertical Agency: Account Profile

## Operating context

- Specialized agency serving a clear vertical, buyer type, compliance context, or operational niche.
- The agency often has strong patterns from many similar clients but lacks a structured way to turn those patterns into reusable intelligence.
- Clients expect domain fluency and practical guidance, not generic creative language.

## What this means for content

For this segment, content is not only a writing task. It is a coordination problem. The team needs approved context, buyer understanding, review discipline, and a shared way to decide what should be said.

## Decision environment

The founder or strategy lead decides when the system strengthens the agency's domain expertise and protects client-specific context.
