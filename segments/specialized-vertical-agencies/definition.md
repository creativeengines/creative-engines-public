# Specialized Vertical Agency: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Agencies focused on a specific industry, buyer type, or service niche where domain knowledge and repeatable expertise are the main competitive advantages.

## Boundaries

- Specialized agency serving a clear vertical, buyer type, compliance context, or operational niche.
- The agency often has strong patterns from many similar clients but lacks a structured way to turn those patterns into reusable intelligence.
- Clients expect domain fluency and practical guidance, not generic creative language.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from broad agencies because the agency wins through vertical fluency and repeatable domain-specific insight.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
