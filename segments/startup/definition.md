# Startup: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Early-stage to growth-stage startups that need sharper positioning, buyer understanding, and content discipline while the product and market are still changing.

## Boundaries

- Founder-led company with changing product, changing market assumptions, and limited content capacity.
- The team needs to learn quickly from buyer reactions while avoiding generic startup messaging.
- Content often has to support fundraising narratives, sales conversations, launch material, and early category education.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from mid-market SaaS because many assumptions are still unstable and founder context remains central.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
