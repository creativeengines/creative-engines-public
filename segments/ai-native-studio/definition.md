# AI-Native Creative Studio: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

AI-native studios that build services, workflows, or creative systems around AI but still need strategy, brand intelligence, and buyer understanding above generation.

## Boundaries

- Studio or hybrid agency/product team built around AI-enabled creative, strategy, or content workflows.
- The team may be highly technical and fast-moving, but still needs credible strategy, buyer understanding, and brand coherence.
- The studio often sells sophistication, so shallow AI output is especially damaging.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from traditional agencies because AI is already central to the operating model; different from Creative Engines because many studios focus on execution while Creative Engines focuses on governance and intelligence above execution.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
