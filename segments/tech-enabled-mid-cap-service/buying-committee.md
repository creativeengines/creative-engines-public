# Tech-Enabled Mid-Cap Service Business: Buying Committee

## Titles commonly involved

- Founder / CEO
- COO
- CMO / VP Marketing
- Head of Growth
- VP Sales / CRO
- Head of Engineering / CTO
- VP Customer Success / VP Operations
- CFO / Controller
- VP People / Head of HR

## Decision-process summary

The practical center is usually operations plus technology, with marketing or growth translating the need into market-facing work. The CEO approves when the system supports clearer positioning, stronger trust, and operational leverage.

The exact path depends on company structure, but the pattern is consistent: one team feels the pain first, several stakeholders need confidence, and the system must be understood as strategy and governance support rather than a shortcut around human judgment.
