# Tech-Enabled Mid-Cap Service Business: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Scaled service businesses where technology supports delivery, operations, quality, or measurement, but the company is not primarily a SaaS product company.

## Boundaries

- Operator-led service company with internal engineering, operations, customer-success, growth, and leadership teams.
- Technology supports delivery quality, workflow, insight, or operational leverage, but human expertise and service trust remain central.
- The company often has serious internal tools, but content and positioning still depend on expert context and buyer understanding.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from B2B SaaS because the service is the product; different from agencies because the company markets and sells its own service rather than client work.

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
