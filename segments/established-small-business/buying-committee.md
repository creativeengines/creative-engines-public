# Established Small Business: Buying Committee

## Titles commonly involved

- Owner / CEO
- Fractional CMO or Marketing Consultant
- Marketing Manager
- Operations Lead
- Sales Lead
- External Agency or Specialist

## Decision-process summary

The owner or operating leader usually decides, often influenced by a fractional marketing advisor or trusted external specialist who needs better strategic handoff and execution clarity.

The exact path depends on company structure, but the pattern is consistent: one team feels the pain first, several stakeholders need confidence, and the system must be understood as strategy and governance support rather than a shortcut around human judgment.
