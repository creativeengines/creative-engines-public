# Bootstrapped Micro-SaaS / Indie ISV: Account Profile

## Operating context

- Small software company with founder-led product, marketing, support, and customer insight.
- The team may have strong product craft but limited time for positioning, content, and buyer research.
- The founder needs content that sounds direct, useful, and authentic rather than bloated or enterprise-coded.

## What this means for content

For this segment, content is not only a writing task. It is a coordination problem. The team needs approved context, buyer understanding, review discipline, and a shared way to decide what should be said.

## Decision environment

The founder usually decides directly after judging whether the system respects their voice, product nuance, and preference for practical usefulness.
