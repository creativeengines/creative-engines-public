# Bootstrapped Micro-SaaS / Indie ISV: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Independent software founders and small product teams that need sharper positioning, buyer clarity, and content leverage without adopting heavy enterprise workflows.

## Boundaries

- Small software company with founder-led product, marketing, support, and customer insight.
- The team may have strong product craft but limited time for positioning, content, and buyer research.
- The founder needs content that sounds direct, useful, and authentic rather than bloated or enterprise-coded.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from startups because the company often prioritizes independence, profitability, and direct customer learning over venture-style growth signaling.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
