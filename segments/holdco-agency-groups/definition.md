# Holdco Agency Group: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Large agency holding-company environments where Creative Engines is more likely to fit as an enabling or partner layer than as a direct replacement for internal platforms.

## Boundaries

- Global or multi-network agency group with internal AI platforms, centralized client systems, creative agencies, media agencies, transformation teams, and procurement structures.
- The organization has massive internal capability but still faces local-client complexity, brand-governance questions, and strategy handoff drift.
- Individual teams may need better methods even when the group has its own tooling strategy.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from independent agencies because the organization already has major internal platforms, transformation mandates, and centralized technology agendas.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
