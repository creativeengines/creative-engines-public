# Independent Web Dev / WordPress Agency: Pain Points

**Public fit group:** Not For

## Core pain points

- Project work is feast-or-famine, with uneven pipeline and scope creep.
- Clients ask for content and marketing results the shop is not set up to own.
- Maintenance and support consume the hours that could go to higher-value work.
- Differentiation is hard when many shops offer similar build-and-maintain services.

## Why Creative Engines is not the primary fit

These pains are mostly about delivery capacity, technical maintenance, and pipeline. Creative Engines addresses a different problem: turning buyer understanding and brand strategy into governed content. It does not solve site delivery or hosting, which is where this segment usually needs help first.

## Where it could become relevant

If the shop deliberately moves into ongoing client content, positioning, or strategy services, Creative Engines can support that newer line of work. Until then, a delivery-focused tool is the better fit.
