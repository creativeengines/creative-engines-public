# Mid-Market DTC / Founder-Led Premium Brand: Buying Committee

## Titles commonly involved

- Founder / CEO
- VP Brand
- Head of Growth
- Creative Director
- Embedded Boutique Agency Partner
- CMO or Marketing Lead

## Decision-process summary

The founder or brand leader often holds final voice authority, while growth and creative stakeholders evaluate whether the system can protect distinctiveness while supporting performance work.

The exact path depends on company structure, but the pattern is consistent: one team feels the pain first, several stakeholders need confidence, and the system must be understood as strategy and governance support rather than a shortcut around human judgment.
