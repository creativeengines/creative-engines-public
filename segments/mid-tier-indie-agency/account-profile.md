# Mid-Tier Independent Agency: Account Profile

## Operating context

- Independent agency with strategy, creative, account, innovation, finance, and delivery functions.
- The agency competes with holdcos, boutiques, AI-native studios, and internal client teams.
- Senior strategy and creative judgment remain valuable, but AI pressure changes how clients perceive speed, value, and staffing.
- The agency needs to protect craft and strategic depth while productizing parts of the work system.

## What this means for content

For this segment, content is not only a writing task. It is a coordination problem. The team needs approved context, buyer understanding, review discipline, and a shared way to decide what should be said.

## Decision environment

The decision is founder-led or strategy-led, but creative authority and finance discipline can block adoption if the system feels like a volume tool rather than a craft and strategy amplifier.
