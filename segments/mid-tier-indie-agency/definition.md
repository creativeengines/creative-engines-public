# Mid-Tier Independent Agency: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Independent agencies large enough to manage serious clients and complex strategy, but still close enough to delivery that margin, quality, and senior-talent leverage matter every week.

## Boundaries

- Independent agency with strategy, creative, account, innovation, finance, and delivery functions.
- The agency competes with holdcos, boutiques, AI-native studios, and internal client teams.
- Senior strategy and creative judgment remain valuable, but AI pressure changes how clients perceive speed, value, and staffing.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from boutique agencies because it has larger teams, more formal client structures, and a stronger need to preserve strategy across departments.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
