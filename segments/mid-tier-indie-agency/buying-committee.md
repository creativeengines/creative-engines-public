# Mid-Tier Independent Agency: Buying Committee

## Titles commonly involved

- Founder / CEO / Managing Partner
- Chief Creative Officer
- Head of Innovation / Head of AI Practice
- Chief Financial Officer
- Senior Account Director
- Strategy Director / Head of Planning
- Programmatic or AdTech Lead

## Decision-process summary

The decision is founder-led or strategy-led, but creative authority and finance discipline can block adoption if the system feels like a volume tool rather than a craft and strategy amplifier.

The exact path depends on company structure, but the pattern is consistent: one team feels the pain first, several stakeholders need confidence, and the system must be understood as strategy and governance support rather than a shortcut around human judgment.
