# Mid-Cap Enterprise Brand: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Scaled enterprise brands below the largest global tier, often with multiple markets, locations, product lines, or business units, but without the internal infrastructure of the largest corporations.

## Boundaries

- Multi-location, regional, industrial, professional-services, franchise, or mid-cap brand with meaningful internal marketing operations.
- The team has enough scale to need governance, but not enough specialist capacity to solve everything internally.
- Marketing, sales, operations, finance, and external partners frequently influence content direction.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from F500 brands because they have comparable complexity but less internal capacity to build custom brand-intelligence systems.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
