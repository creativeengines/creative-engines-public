# Mid-Cap Enterprise Brand: Buying Committee

## Titles commonly involved

- CMO
- VP Marketing / Head of Brand
- Marketing Operations Lead
- CFO
- Head of Digital / Performance Marketing
- External Agency Partner
- Director of Franchise / Local Marketing
- Managing Partner / Practice Group Lead
- CIO
- RevOps Administrator
- VP Sales / CRO
- Controller / Director of Finance

## Decision-process summary

The decision often begins in marketing but becomes cross-functional when content supports sales, local execution, customer education, or portfolio consistency.

The exact path depends on company structure, but the pattern is consistent: one team feels the pain first, several stakeholders need confidence, and the system must be understood as strategy and governance support rather than a shortcut around human judgment.
