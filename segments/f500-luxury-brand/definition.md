# F500 Luxury Brand: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Large luxury, heritage, beauty, fashion, jewelry, spirits, automotive, or ultra-premium brands where taste, discretion, craft codes, and brand aura dominate content decisions.

## Boundaries

- Brand organization with strong creative authority, heritage codes, regional teams, archive material, and highly protected visual and verbal identity.
- Decision-making is slower when content touches prestige, founder heritage, maison codes, or high-visibility campaigns.
- Digital and transformation teams may test AI, but creative leadership needs control over what enters the brand surface.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from other F500 brands because visible AI volume can threaten prestige; the useful role for AI is quiet support for coherence, review, and controlled digital expression.

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
