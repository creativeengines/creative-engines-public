# F500 Luxury Brand: Buying Committee

## Titles commonly involved

- Global Creative Director / Image Director
- Brand Custodian
- CMO / Global Communications Director
- Head of Digital Transformation
- Heritage Archive / IP Manager
- Procurement Director
- CFO / VP Finance
- CIO / Head of Digital Infrastructure

## Decision-process summary

The decision is creative-authority-led. Digital or marketing leaders may sponsor the initiative, but the system must win trust from the people who protect the brand codes.

The exact path depends on company structure, but the pattern is consistent: one team feels the pain first, several stakeholders need confidence, and the system must be understood as strategy and governance support rather than a shortcut around human judgment.
