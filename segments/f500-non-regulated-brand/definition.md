# F500 Non-Regulated Brand: Definition and Boundaries

## Segment definition

Large consumer, business, industrial, retail, technology, media, or travel brands with broad audiences and complex brand systems, but without the heaviest regulated-review burden.

## Boundaries

- Global or multi-region brand organization with several brand, product, or market teams.
- Internal creative, media, insight, operations, legal, and procurement stakeholders all influence content decisions.
- The company often already uses AI-assisted production somewhere, but the harder problem is keeping brand coherence, buyer relevance, and executive confidence across many teams.

## How it differs from other segments

Different from regulated F500 segments because brand governance, scale, and internal alignment are the main constraints rather than formal compliance review.

## Public boundary

This page describes the public buyer segment. It does not expose internal prioritization labels, financial assumptions, named target accounts, source research mechanics, or protected methodology details.
