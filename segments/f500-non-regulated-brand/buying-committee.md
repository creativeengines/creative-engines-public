# F500 Non-Regulated Brand: Buying Committee

## Titles commonly involved

- Global CMO
- SVP / VP Brand
- Brand Director
- Head of In-House Creative Studio
- Marketing Procurement Lead
- Brand Trust Counsel
- Director of Brand Effectiveness / Insights
- AOR Account Lead
- CFO / VP Finance
- CIO / Head of Enterprise Data Governance
- VP Marketing Operations
- VP Media / Programmatic Buying Lead

## Decision-process summary

The decision is usually coalition-led. Brand and marketing leaders sponsor the need, operations and data leaders check whether the system can fit into existing work, legal or trust teams review risk boundaries, and finance/procurement pressure the team to justify why this is more than another content tool.

The exact path depends on company structure, but the pattern is consistent: one team feels the pain first, several stakeholders need confidence, and the system must be understood as strategy and governance support rather than a shortcut around human judgment.
